# PR Agency User Research — Influencer Campaign Workflow

> A 5–7 minute survey to understand how Indian PR agencies run influencer
> campaigns today, what hurts most, and what a better system would look like.
> Honest answers shape the v1 of the platform — and pilot agencies get free
> white-glove onboarding.

Drop these questions into Google Forms / Tally / Typeform. Each line marks the
field type in `[brackets]`. Sections marked `(optional)` can be skipped without
breaking the survey logic.

## Suggested form settings

- **Length on the form footer:** "~5–7 minutes"
- **Required questions:** Q2, Q4, Q5, Q9, Q10, Q15, Q21, Q24, Q30. Mark everything else optional — completion rate matters more than completeness.
- **Conditional logic** (Typeform / Tally support this):
  - If Q21 includes "Through the brand directly", skip Q22 + Q23.
  - If Q30 = "Not right now", skip Q31 + Q32.
- **Page break** after each section heading. Three short pages outperform one long scroll.
- **Privacy line at the top:** "We'll only use these answers to design the product. Nothing is shared with brands or other agencies."

## Distribution checklist

- Post in 2–3 PR / marketing Slack communities you're in.
- DM 10 agency founders you already know personally — best signal-to-noise.
- LinkedIn post: keep it short, share the same form link.
- Offer something small in return for completion: a copy of the synthesised findings, or 30-min strategy call.
- Target: 20 completed responses before drawing conclusions. 30+ if you can.

## After responses come in

- Quantitative answers go in a sheet — compute distributions for Q5, Q12, Q13, Q15, Q24, Q28.
- Qualitative answers (Q11, Q16, Q29) — tag for themes; surface the 3 most common.
- Cross-tab Q15 ("most painful step") against Q5 ("campaigns per month") — pain often scales non-linearly with volume.
- Anyone who picks "Yes — please contact me" on Q30 gets a calendar link within 24h.
